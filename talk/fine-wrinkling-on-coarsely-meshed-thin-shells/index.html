    Fine wrinkling on Coarsely Meshed Thin Shells

    Zhen Chen, Hsiao-Yu Chen, Danny M. Kaufman, Mélina Skouras, Etienne Vouga
    Fast Forward Pre-recording Presentation

    Abstract

    We propose a new model and algorithm to capture the high-definition statics of thin shells via coarse meshes. This model predicts global, fine-scale wrinkling at frequencies much higher than the resolution of the coarse mesh
